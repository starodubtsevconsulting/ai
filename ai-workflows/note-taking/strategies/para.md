# PARA Strategy

## Purpose

Organize information primarily by actionability and current context using Projects, Areas, Resources and Archives.

## Structure

- Projects — active outcomes with an end state.
- Areas — ongoing responsibilities/standards without a fixed end date.
- Resources — topics/material that may be useful.
- Archives — inactive material from the other categories.

## Principles

- Organize for retrieval/use rather than perfect subject classification.
- Allow material to move as its relevance changes.
- Keep active information close to current projects/areas.
- Archive aggressively enough that active spaces remain useful.

## Example flow

`capture -> determine current actionability/context -> Project/Area/Resource -> use/review -> move/archive as context changes`

Concrete folders/apps and naming conventions belong to runtime/profile configuration.
