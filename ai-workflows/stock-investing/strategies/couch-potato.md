# Couch Potato Strategy

## Purpose

A passive, long-term investing strategy based on the well-known Canadian Couch Potato approach: keep the portfolio simple, broadly diversified, low-cost and disciplined rather than trying to predict individual stock winners or short-term market movements.

This is a strategy for the Stock Investing workflow, not a separate workflow and not a promise of investment performance.

## Core principles

- Use broad-market, low-cost index funds/ETFs rather than relying on individual-stock selection.
- Diversify across major asset classes and geographies appropriate to the investor's plan.
- Choose a target asset allocation that reflects risk capacity, time horizon and objectives.
- Contribute consistently rather than attempting to time market entries.
- Rebalance periodically or when allocation drift crosses defined thresholds.
- Minimize unnecessary trading, fees, taxes and behavioral reactions to market noise.
- Prefer a simple portfolio that can actually be maintained over a theoretically optimal but fragile/complex one.
- Change the strategic allocation because goals/circumstances changed, not merely because markets moved recently.

## Strategist responsibilities

The Workflow Strategist should preserve the selected target allocation, rationale, permitted instruments/categories, contribution/rebalancing policy, relevant account/tax constraints supplied by the private profile, and evidence behind material strategy changes.

The Strategist should distinguish:

- strategic allocation changes from ordinary rebalancing;
- contributions from market-driven allocation drift;
- evidence-based changes from performance chasing;
- portfolio-level decisions from short-term trading signals.

## Example flow

`goal/risk constraints -> target allocation -> select broad low-cost instruments -> contribute -> observe allocation drift -> rebalance when policy requires -> periodic strategy review`

Market news alone should not automatically create a portfolio action. Most events should result in observation/no action unless they affect the investor's goals, constraints, instrument suitability, or predefined rebalancing rules.

## Relationship to Global Governor

The Stock Investing Strategist manages HOW this strategy operates inside investing. The Global Governor owns broader questions such as WHY capital is being invested, how much capital/time investing receives relative to other goals, major liquidity needs, and relevant human behavior/context.

## Implementation note

Concrete ETF/fund selections, target percentages, account types, tax treatment and rebalancing thresholds belong to private profile/runtime configuration or a separately reviewed strategy specialization. They should not be inferred as universally appropriate from this generic strategy contract.
