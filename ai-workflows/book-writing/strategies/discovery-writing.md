# Discovery Writing Strategy

A book-writing strategy where substantial structure is discovered through drafting rather than fully fixed before prose begins.

The Workflow Strategist may choose this when exploration, character/idea discovery, voice, or emergent structure is more valuable than early architectural certainty.

This strategy influences the concrete flow: drafting may precede detailed outlining; discoveries can trigger structural updates; continuity/review becomes important as the manuscript grows.

It is a strategy input, not a universal pipeline. The Strategist adapts it to the particular book and evidence from the work.
