# Outline-First Strategy

A book-writing strategy that invests in structure before sustained drafting.

The Workflow Strategist may choose this when argument order, plot architecture, research coverage, dependencies, or delivery predictability benefit from stronger upfront structure.

This strategy influences the concrete flow: premise and research may lead to outline validation before chapter drafting, with later evidence still allowed to change the structure.

It is a strategy input, not a fixed universal pipeline. The Strategist adapts it to the particular book.
