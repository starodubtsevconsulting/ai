# SEO Content Strategy

## Purpose

Develop useful articles around demonstrated search demand while preserving editorial quality and avoiding content created only to satisfy keywords.

## Principles

- Start from a real audience question/search intent.
- Research the topic and competing coverage before choosing the angle.
- Prefer information gain: add evidence, explanation, examples or perspective not already obvious from existing results.
- Match structure and depth to the reader's intent rather than mechanically maximizing length or keyword frequency.
- Maintain factual provenance and editorial voice.
- Publish, observe search/discovery evidence, and improve or consolidate content when evidence warrants it.
- Avoid producing near-duplicate pages solely to target minor keyword variants.

## Strategist behavior

The Workflow Strategist should remember topic coverage, search-intent assumptions, content overlap, performance evidence and updates so future selection does not repeatedly recreate the same article.

## Example flow

`search/audience evidence -> topic opportunity -> intent/competition research -> angle -> draft -> factual/editorial review -> publish -> observe discovery evidence -> update/consolidate/follow-up`
