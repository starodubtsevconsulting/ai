# Value Investing Strategy

## Purpose

A long-term stock-investing strategy centered on buying businesses when the market price appears meaningfully below a defensible estimate of underlying value, while requiring enough margin for uncertainty and analytical error.

This contrasts intentionally with the Couch Potato strategy: Couch Potato generally avoids selecting individual winners and owns broad markets; Value Investing requires selective company-level research and explicit valuation/thesis work.

## Core principles

- Treat a stock as an ownership interest in a business, not merely a moving price.
- Understand the business, economics, industry, competitive position and major risks before forming a thesis.
- Estimate value using appropriate fundamental evidence rather than price momentum alone.
- Require a margin of safety between estimated value and purchase price to account for uncertainty.
- Prefer understandable businesses and evidence that can be independently checked.
- Distinguish temporary market pessimism from genuine deterioration in business value.
- Avoid buying merely because a stock looks statistically cheap; investigate why it is cheap.
- Revisit the thesis when material evidence changes rather than reacting automatically to ordinary price volatility.
- Judge the quality of the decision process separately from short-term market outcomes.

## Strategist responsibilities

The Workflow Strategist should preserve for each candidate/holding:

- investment thesis;
- key assumptions;
- valuation method and range;
- margin-of-safety requirement;
- business/industry evidence and provenance;
- risks and thesis invalidation conditions;
- decision rationale;
- subsequent evidence and outcomes;
- lessons from completed investments and rejected candidates.

The Strategist should explicitly distinguish facts, estimates, assumptions and conclusions so later sessions can reconsider the original reasoning.

## Example flow

`candidate -> business research -> financial/industry analysis -> valuation -> risk/thesis review -> margin-of-safety decision -> buy/pass/watch -> monitor material evidence -> hold/reassess/exit -> post-decision review`

Price movement by itself does not necessarily invalidate the thesis. Material changes in fundamentals, assumptions, valuation, risk or opportunity cost may.

## Relationship to Global Governor

The Stock Investing Strategist owns HOW Value Investing is applied inside this workflow. The Global Governor owns broader allocation questions: WHY capital is invested, how much risk/capital belongs here relative to other goals/assets, liquidity needs, and relevant human context.

## Implementation note

Valuation methods, required margins of safety, portfolio concentration, eligible securities, account/tax constraints and execution permissions are profile/strategy specializations. They must not be assumed universally appropriate from this generic contract.
