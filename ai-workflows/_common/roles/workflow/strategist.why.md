# Why Workflow Strategist?

A workflow needs somebody who remembers how that particular domain is supposed to work over time.

The Global Governor can say where the Human wants to go, but it should not need to carry the detailed strategy of software development, accounting, multimedia production or every other workflow. Execution agents, meanwhile, are usually too bounded and short-lived to own that continuity.

Workflow Strategist fills the middle layer.

It owns the durable **HOW** for one workflow: the strategies, context and domain continuity that should survive individual tasks and sessions. It can advise the Human or the workflow team and communicate upward when broader direction is needed.

This keeps global goals separate from domain strategy:

`Human / Global Governor -> what matters`

`Workflow Strategist -> how this workflow approaches it`

`Execution team -> do the bounded work`

Without that layer, either the global agent becomes overloaded with every domain detail or each execution session has to rediscover the workflow's strategy again.
