# Why Admin?

![Admin role visual](img/admin.png)

Sometimes the workflow itself gets stuck.

An agent may need to be recreated, an ID may become stale, initialization may be incomplete, or the normal team rules may make recovery awkward. Asking another ordinary agent to break those rules would defeat the point of having the rules.

Admin is the Human's operational recovery door into a workflow.

It can initialize the team, replace or retire agents, repair the runtime roster and restore a valid working state. Other agents cannot use Admin as a shortcut around their own authority; Admin exists for the Human to operate the workflow itself.

Think of it less as the team's boss and more as the maintenance access panel. Most of the time nobody needs to open it. When the machinery is stuck, it is useful to know exactly where the panel is.
