# Stock Investing Workflow

## Purpose

Support deliberate stock-market investment research and portfolio decision-making over longer horizons. The workflow preserves investment theses, assumptions, evidence, valuation/risk reasoning, decisions and outcomes so that strategy can improve rather than repeatedly restarting from current market noise.

## Strategic layer

The Workflow Strategist owns `WORKFLOW_STRATEGIC` investment memory. It may maintain theses, watch conditions, decision rationale, portfolio constraints, risk assumptions, rejected opportunities, post-decision evidence and lessons.

The Strategist determines HOW the investing workflow approaches research and portfolio decisions. WHY capital should be allocated to investing at all, WHEN investing deserves attention relative to other activities, and the human's broader financial/life context belong to the Global Governor.

## Strategies

Different investment strategies may produce substantially different flows: value, growth, quality, dividend/income, factor-based, concentrated, diversified, event-driven or custom approaches. Strategy is explicit and may be changed when evidence invalidates assumptions.

Current examples include `couch-potato.md` and `value-investing.md` under `strategies/`.

## Candidate execution roles

Possible ephemeral `SESSION` roles include Researcher, Company Analyst, Industry Analyst, Valuation Analyst, Risk Reviewer and Portfolio Reviewer.

## Events and adaptive flow

Meaningful events may include a candidate entering the research universe, thesis creation, new financial results, valuation changes, material company/industry events, thesis invalidation, portfolio review, buy/hold/reduce/exit decisions and post-decision review.

The Strategist connects these events into a concrete flow appropriate to the selected investment strategy.

## Evidence and decision boundary

Research should preserve provenance to source material and clearly separate observed facts, assumptions, estimates and strategic conclusions. Historical outcomes should be retained so the workflow can evaluate whether its reasoning process is improving.

The workflow is a decision-support/process contract, not a guarantee of investment performance. Concrete brokerage credentials, private holdings and execution permissions belong to private profile/runtime configuration.

## Runtime boundary

The workflow remains independent of a particular broker, market-data vendor, model, harness or provider. Runtime/profile configuration supplies authorized data sources, portfolio context, tools and any permitted execution integrations.
