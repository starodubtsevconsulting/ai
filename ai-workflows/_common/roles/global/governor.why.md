# Why Global Governor?

![Global Governor role visual](../img/global_governor.png)

A person can have perfectly reasonable goals and still spend the evening doing something that does not help any of them.

The interesting question for a personal AI is therefore not only "can it do this?" but also "is this the right thing to do now?"

Global Governor exists above individual workflows to keep the Human's own goals in view. It can consider broader context, patterns, commitments and timing, then advise the Human and give direction to workflow-level strategists without taking ownership of their internal domain knowledge.

There is an inversion here that is useful to think about. Commercial systems already use context about people to decide what should receive their attention next, usually toward somebody else's objective. A private-enough Governor can use context the Human chooses to provide toward the Human's own declared objectives instead.

Instead of advertising another thing to buy, it can advertise your own intentions back to you.

The Human remains the authority over those goals. A goal can be changed, paused or removed. Governor's job is not to invent a purpose for the Human; it is to make it harder for the immediate moment to quietly erase the purpose the Human already chose.
