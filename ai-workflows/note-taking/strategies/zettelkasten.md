# Zettelkasten Strategy

## Purpose

Develop knowledge through small, independently understandable notes connected by meaningful relationships rather than merely collecting information in folders.

## Principles

- Capture ideas in your own words when possible.
- Prefer focused/atomic notes that can stand independently.
- Preserve source/reference information where relevant.
- Link notes because of meaningful conceptual relationships, not simply because they share a topic label.
- Let structure emerge from accumulated connections rather than requiring a complete taxonomy upfront.
- Revisit and refine notes as understanding develops.
- Use the network of notes to support new arguments, writing and discovery.

## Example flow

`capture/source -> understand/distill -> create focused note -> connect to existing notes -> revisit/refine -> use connections to develop new work`

The concrete conventions for identifiers, filenames, links, indexes and storage belong to private profile/runtime configuration.
